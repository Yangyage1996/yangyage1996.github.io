About

I am a PhD student at Beijing Jiaotong University. My research focuses on human–computer interaction and human–automation function allocation for intelligent dispatching in urban rail transit.

I study how dispatchers and automation can share information-processing and decision-making tasks, and how their collaboration affects task performance, mental workload, and situation awareness during emergency response.

我是杨亚格，北京交通大学博士研究生。面向城市轨道交通智能调度，主要研究人机交互与人机功能分配，关注调度员与自动化系统的协作，以及自动化对任务绩效、心理负荷和情境意识的影响。

02

Research

I

Human–computer interaction

Studying how people interact with automated systems and evaluating the effects of automation on performance, workload, and situation awareness.

II

Human–automation function allocation

Examining which information-processing and decision-making activities can be assigned to automation, and how allocation depends on the demands of the task.

III

Intelligent dispatching in urban rail transit

Applying human factors methods to the design and evaluation of intelligent dispatching systems, with a focus on dispatcher–automation collaboration during emergency response.
